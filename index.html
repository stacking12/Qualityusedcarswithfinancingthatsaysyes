Frequently Asked Questions

Buy Here Pay Here, explained.

What does "Buy Here Pay Here" actually mean?

It means the dealership handles your financing directly, in-house, instead of sending your application to a bank or outside lender. You buy the car and make your payments right here at the dealership, which is why approvals are faster and more flexible.

I have bad credit or no credit. Can I still get approved?

Yes. We accept all credit situations, including bad credit, no credit, or past repossessions. Our approval decisions are based on your ability to make payments today, not your credit history.

How fast can I get approved and drive a car home?

Most customers receive same-day approval. In many cases, you can complete the paperwork and drive away in your quality used car the same day you visit.

Do you offer Spanish-speaking staff?

Yes, our team includes Spanish-speaking staff to walk you through the entire process in the language you're most comfortable with.

Do the used cars come with any kind of warranty?

Yes, our quality used cars are backed by a dealer warranty for added peace of mind after your purchase.
